# Combat tuning of major non-Halo shooters (cross-genre baseline, as of 2026-11-09)

Reading notes for the report writer:
- "calc." means I derived the number arithmetically from the cited inputs (shots-to-kill minus one, times the shot interval). These are optimal, zero-miss, no-falloff TTKs measured from the first hit. They are listed under Inferences unless the source itself published the TTK.
- Many primary pages (Liquipedia, PC Gamer, GamesRadar, Fandom, EA forums, Unreal Engine blog) refused direct fetches. Where a fact comes only from a search-result summary of such a page, it is marked "(search summary)". Treat those as lower confidence than fetched pages.
- Unit conversions are a trap: the Apex wiki implies 1 unit = 1 inch (39.37 units per metre), while the Titanfall wiki and the Titanfall 1 datamine imply 1 unit = 0.75 inch (1.905 cm). Both games are on the same engine family. Every m/s figure for a Source-family game below states which convention it uses.

## Time-to-kill: pools, damage, optimal TTK, classification, and reactions to TTK changes

### Takeaway
The field splits into a short-TTK cluster (CS2, Valorant, Call of Duty, Titanfall 2: one headshot or roughly 200-300 ms) and a long-TTK cluster (Apex, The Finals, Overwatch 2, Destiny 2, Quake vs a stacked player, Splitgate: roughly 0.7-1.5 s), and every long-TTK game has moved its TTK at least once with a divided community reaction. Developers who shortened TTK did it to stop mobility/escape from dominating (Apex S24) or to avoid a "spongy" feel (Black Ops 7); developers who lengthened it did it to cut burst deaths and give time to react (Overwatch 2 S9, Splitgate 2 playtest and the Arena Reloaded relaunch).

#### TTK changes, stated reasons, and reactions

- Apex Season 24 (Feb 2025): Respawn buffed every weapon, removed white/blue/purple helmets, made headshot damage a fixed per-weapon value, and capped armour at purple. A year later (Season 28 designers' notes) they explained that higher TTK led players to "value mobility too much over tactical positioning", that they "actively referenced" earlier lower-TTK seasons, and that "it was a deliberate decision to bring the TTK down mainly through the weapons themselves instead of just armor and health"; they now call TTK "in a good spot". — [EA designers' notes 28.0](https://www.ea.com/games/apex-legends/apex-legends/news/28-0-designers-notes), [PCGamesN S24 notes](https://www.pcgamesn.com/apex-legends/season-24-patch-notes), [Dexerto](https://dexerto.com/apex-legends/apex-legends-makes-huge-changes-to-shield-helmets-and-healing-in-season-24-3057202)
- Apex S24 reaction was split: some players asked for a revert ("The community deserves better"), others said it made the game easier to learn; a Steam thread argued "TTK didn't fix 3rd party - it made it worse". Respawn did not revert. — [Sportskeeda: divided](https://sportskeeda.com/esports/community-divided-new-ttk-changes-apex-legends), [Sportskeeda: revert calls](https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/the-community-deserves-better-apex-legends-players-want-new-ttk-changes-reverted), [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1172470/discussions/0/604149124789492584)
- Apex also shortened the healing side at the same time: shield cell use 3 s -> 2.5 s, cells and syringes stack to 6, players start with 4 of each (was 2). — [Shield Cell wiki](https://apexlegends.wiki.gg/wiki/Shield_Cell)
- Overwatch 2 Season 9 (13 Feb 2024): bigger projectiles (see weapon-handling section), +15-25% health for everyone, a universal regen passive, and a DPS passive cutting healing received by 20% for 2 s. Coverage described the goal as improving hit consistency and reducing the impact of burst damage and healing. — [Blizzard patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2024/02), [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/overwatch/overwatch-2-season-9-gameplay-changes-explained/) (search summary)
- Overwatch 2 S9 reaction: described as its "most controversial" update; players worried it made the game "too easy to play"; a forum revert thread complained higher health "smooths out burst kills ... requiring more follow-up to get a deserved kill". Eight days later Blizzard shrank several projectile sizes again; lead hero designer Alec Dawson said "accuracy numbers are meeting intentions but some are pushed a bit too far". — [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/news/overwatch-2-season-9-update-controversial-mixed-reviews), [Blizzard forum thread](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/overwatch/t/revert-s9-changes/1011717), [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/overwatch/overwatch-2-gets-projectile-size-and-hero-health-adjustments)
- Destiny 2 Update 7.3.5 (Mar 2024, first PvP Strike Team sandbox pass): +30 base health; primaries shifted toward precision (pulse/auto/sidearm/scout +14% crit damage; hand cannons +10% crit and -5% body; SMGs +12.5% crit and -3% body); Crucible-only ability cooldown penalties of 15% (melee, grenade, class) and 20% (super). Press called it the biggest PvP sandbox shake-up since double primaries, in response to a Crucible "in a downward spiral". — [Shacknews patch notes](https://www.shacknews.com/article/139002/destiny-2-update-7-3-5-patch-notes), [Shacknews Strike Team piece](https://shacknews.com/article/138839/destiny-2-pvp-strike-team-has-fixes-for-crucible) (search summary)
- Black Ops 7 (2025): the design director said TTK is "currently sitting around Black Ops 6 TTK", framed by GamesRadar as a choice "to stop it feeling overly spongy". — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/call-of-duty/call-of-duty-black-ops-7-ttk-is-similar-to-black-ops-6-to-stop-it-feeling-overly-spongy-devs-hope-to-have-cold-war-style-health-bars-ready-for-the-beta)
- Black Ops 6: one outlet says the slower TTK "gives omnimovement more room to shine"; another Dexerto headline says the developers confirmed a TTK faster than MW3 and Cold War. The two reports conflict. — [CharlieIntel](https://charlieintel.com/call-of-duty/black-ops-6-devs-reveal-how-slow-ttk-compares-to-bocw-336741); contradicted by [Dexerto](https://dexerto.com/call-of-duty/black-ops-6-devs-confirms-faster-time-to-kill-speed-than-mw3-and-black-ops-cold-war-2880526)
- Warzone 2: analyst JGOD argued a blanket health increase would not fix TTK complaints because long-range TTK was already equal to Warzone 1's; the problem was close-range SMGs killing too fast, so the fix is one more shot-to-kill on those guns. Earlier, Warzone players attacked a planned TTK increase for "reducing the skill gap". — [CharlieIntel: JGOD](https://www.charlieintel.com/call-of-duty-warzone/jgod-explains-how-increasing-health-wont-fix-warzone-2s-ttk-problem-219055/), [CharlieIntel: skill gap](https://www.charlieintel.com/call-of-duty-warzone/warzone-players-slam-raven-for-reducing-skill-gap-with-time-to-kill-increase-plans-156035/)
- The Finals "Respec Order" experiment (10.3.0, 16 Apr 2026): Light 150 -> 175, Heavy 350 -> 325, Light about 10% slower, Heavy about 10% faster. Embark's Matt Lowe: the 150-350 spread is "probably a little too wide", making weapons hard to balance across body types, and Lights arrive at fights 5-10 seconds before Heavies, hurting team cohesion. It was announced as a one-week test. — [Respec Order notes](https://www.reachthefinals.com/patchnotes/respec-order), [patched.gg 10.3.0](https://patched.gg/games/the-finals/update-1030)
- Splitgate 2: Public Playtest #1 made TTK 20% longer for all weapons and slightly reduced recoil; the December 2025 relaunch "increased TTK slightly" again to "create more meaningful gunfights and give players more time to react". — [Carbine wiki history](https://splitgatearenareloaded.wiki.gg/wiki/Carbine), [Arena Reloaded launch overview](https://www.splitgate.com/news/splitgate-arena-reloaded-launch-overview)
- Titanfall 2 tech-test impression: TTK "has overall been reduced from the original game but it's still above titles like Call of Duty: Black Ops 3". — [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/?p=276565) (search summary)
- Riot on Valorant: time-to-kill is "extremely low" and most weapons can kill with one well-placed shot. — [Riot: Peeking into Valorant's netcode](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/peeking-valorants-netcode)

- The long-TTK games cluster around 0.7-1.1 s optimal for the default automatic rifle against the default pool. A new shield shooter whose default-weapon optimal TTK sits well above about 1.2 s would be outside the range of every game surveyed here except The Finals vs Heavy and Quake vs a full stack, both of which are deliberately asymmetric situations.
- Two separate developers concluded that the lever for a TTK problem is weapon damage (and specific range bands), not the health pool: Respawn changed weapons "instead of just armor and health", and JGOD's Warzone analysis says a blanket health change would break the range bands that already feel right.
- Reactions follow a pattern: lengthening TTK draws "sponge / deserved kill needs follow-up / skill gap reduced" complaints (Overwatch S9, Warzone); shortening it draws "third parties are worse / less outplay" complaints (Apex S24). Neither direction is safe; the stated reasons matter more than the direction.

### Gaps
- Titanfall 2 pilot health and health-regen numbers: not found in a citable vanilla source (wiki pages retrieved had no numbers; only a mod changelog).
- Overwatch 2 per-hero damage and fire rate (for example Soldier: 76) could not be retrieved (wikis blocked), so no Overwatch TTK in ms is given. Search summaries conflict on Soldier: 76 damage (18 vs 19).
- "Realistic average TTK" (accuracy-adjusted) was not found for any game except the Call of Duty range-bucket tables. No developer published average accuracy alongside TTK.
- Destiny 2 pool after The Edge of Fate (2025) stat rework (Resilience renamed Health) was not confirmed; the 216-230 figure is from March 2024.
- Krunker damage and TTK numbers: no reliable source found.
- Quake Live and Quake Champions exact current health/armour and weapon tables: only third-party DPS comparisons and conflicting guides found.
- Whether The Finals' 175 / 250 / 325 pools are now permanent: the wiki (Oct 2026) and the AKM TTK table (12.2.0) use them, but the only patch note found describes them as a one-week experiment.
- Apex values are from the wiki as edited in early 2026; Seasons 29-30 (mid/late 2026) changes were not checked.

## Movement: speeds, acceleration, strafe reversal, air control, jump, and accuracy penalties

### Takeaway
Tactical shooters pair roughly 5-6 m/s run speeds with a hard accuracy penalty above about 27-34% of top speed, so a duel is "stop, then shoot"; arena and hero shooters keep full or near-full accuracy on the move and rely on target motion (strafes, slides, jumps) to make long TTKs contestable. Acceleration and deceleration constants, the numbers that set how fast a strafe can be reversed, are public only for Quake III (source) and CS2 (cvars and community timing).

#### Acceleration, friction, jump, air control

- Quake III source constants: pm_accelerate 10, pm_airaccelerate 1, pm_friction 6, pm_stopspeed 100, pm_duckScale 0.25; JUMP_VELOCITY 270; STEPSIZE 18. Friction drop per frame = max(speed, stopspeed) x friction x frametime; acceleration adds accel x frametime x wishspeed toward the wish direction, capped at the remaining speed. Air movement uses the same function with acceleration 1, "which gives limited air control" (strafe-jumping falls out of this code path, which the source comments call a bug left enabled). — [bg_pmove.c](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/id-Software/Quake-III-Arena/master/code/game/bg_pmove.c), [bg_local.h](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/id-Software/Quake-III-Arena/master/code/game/bg_local.h)
- CS2 cvars: sv_friction 5.2, sv_stopspeed 80, sv_accelerate 5.5 (one site says 5.6). — [totalcsgo sv_friction](https://totalcsgo.com/commands/svfriction), [totalcsgo sv_stopspeed](https://totalcsgo.com/commands/svstopspeed), [csdb.gg sv_accelerate](https://csdb.gg/commands/server/sv_accelerate); contradicted on accelerate by [totalcsgo](https://totalcsgo.com/commands/svaccelerate)
- CS2 timing (community measurements, not Valve figures): acceleration is front-loaded (0 -> 50 u/s in about 0.032 s but 200 -> 250 takes about 0.27 s; full speed in about half a second). Releasing the key takes about 200 ms to reach accurate speed and about 400 ms (25 ticks) to slide to a stop; a counter-strafe reaches accurate speed in about 80 ms and a full stop in about 110-125 ms. Accuracy returns below roughly 34% of the weapon's top speed (about 73 u/s for the AK). Other guides quote about 88 u/s for rifles. — [TurboSmurfs](https://turbosmurfs.gg/?p=8949), [cs.money](https://blog.cs.money/en/games/how-to-move/) (search summaries)
- CS2 jump: about 54 units standing, about 65 with a crouch-jump; bunny-hop speed is capped near 300 u/s in competitive. Sub-tick input makes the counter-strafe window "marginally wider" than CS:GO. — [csdb.gg movement guide](https://csdb.gg/guides/movement-guide/)
- Valorant accuracy rules: the "deadzone" (speed below which you are accurate) was 25% then 30% of max speed (Patch 0.50) and was cut to 27.5% in Patch 3.0; rifle walking inaccuracy went 1.3 -> 2.0; bullet tagging slow went 75% -> 72.5%. Vandal walk inaccuracy 3, run inaccuracy 6 against a first-shot spread of 0.25. — [Valorant Patch 3.0 notes](https://playvalorant.com/en-sg/news/game-updates/valorant-patch-notes-3-0/), [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/valorant/valorant-run-gun-accuracy-nerfed-episode-3-1598481/) (search summaries), [Hotspawn](https://www.hotspawn.com/?p=91749)
- Riot's reasoning for movement inaccuracy and tagging: weapon accuracy drops while moving, so a peeker must stop or fire inaccurately, and stopping takes time that lets the holder react; a non-lethal hit briefly slows the victim so they are easier to finish. — [Riot netcode article](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/peeking-valorants-netcode)
- Apex spread while moving (R-301 hipfire radius): standing 6.5, walking 8.5, sprinting 9.5, crouching 5, airborne 9.5; ADS spread 0 standing or crouching, 6 airborne. Moving on the ground costs nothing while aiming down sights. — [R-301 wiki](https://apexlegends.wiki.gg/wiki/R-301_Carbine)
- The Finals: moving reduces accuracy, "especially while airborne"; ADS increases accuracy. — [AKM wiki](https://thefinals.wiki/wiki/AKM)
- Krunker slide-hop: jump, crouch in mid-air, slide on landing for a speed boost; there is about a quarter-second window after landing to jump again and keep the speed; the slide always carries you in the direction you face (unlike strafe-jumping). One fan page says friction and jump height were once tied to client frame rate (300+ FPS players slid further) and that a Slide Control setting compensated; a speedrun guide says FPS no longer affects slide speed. — [WePC](https://www.wepc.com/gaming/how-to-slide-hop-in-krunker/), [gamer-guides](https://gamer-guides.com/krunker-io/guide-to-bunnyhop/), [shapes.inc deep dive](https://shapes.inc/fandom/krunker/deep-dive); contradicted by [speedrun.com guide](https://www.speedrun.com/krunker/guide/vm2um)

### Inferences
- Quake III strafe timing, derived from the source constants at 320 u/s (continuous approximation of the per-frame code): standing start to full speed about 0.16 s; coasting to a stop on friction alone about 0.36 s; pressing the opposite key stops you in about 0.08 s and reaches full speed the other way after about 0.23 s total. So a Quake target can fully reverse a strafe roughly four times a second, with full accuracy throughout.
- CS2 by contrast takes about 0.11 s just to stop with a counter-strafe and about 0.5 s to regain full speed, and is inaccurate above a third of top speed. The same input pattern that is a dodge in Quake is a forfeit of accuracy in CS2. A Halo-style game must decide which family it is in; mixing a tactical-shooter acceleration curve (slow reversal) with arena-shooter full accuracy on the move and a long TTK gives targets no dodge and attackers no aim test.
- The conflict between Apex's "5.07 m/s" and "3.8 m/s" walk speeds (and 7.4 vs 5.7 m/s sprint) is purely the inch-per-unit convention (1.0 vs 0.75). The measured 6.5 m/s armed sprint matches the 1-inch convention (299 x 0.87 = 260 u/s = 6.6 m/s). Calc.: Apex armed walk is about 174 u/s (4.4 m/s) and AR ADS strafe about 87 u/s (2.2 m/s) under that convention.
- Apex and Call of Duty both moved, in 2025, toward restricting what you can do with a gun while performing movement tech (Apex lowering TTK because mobility was overvalued; BO7 gating ADS-while-sliding behind a perk). The trend among the big live games is to rein in movement-as-defence slightly, not to add more.

### Gaps
- Ground acceleration, friction and strafe-reversal times for Apex, Overwatch 2, Valorant, Destiny 2, The Finals, Splitgate, Call of Duty and Krunker: no numeric source found. "AD-AD spam" is widely discussed but I found no measured reversal times.
- Jump heights other than CS2 (54 / 65 units) and Quake (jump velocity 270; gravity not retrieved from the source, so height is not computed here).
- Splitgate walk, sprint and jetpack numbers for either game: none found.
- Valorant knife / unarmed run speed and exact stop time: not found in a citable source; one Reddit user test (unverified) says stopping happens at a constant friction rate that inputs cannot shorten.
- Strafe speed relative to forward speed: only Overwatch (backward 90%, low-quality source) was found. Whether Quake applies identical speed in all directions was not verified in the fetched code.
- Overwatch base speed (5.5 m/s) comes from a low-quality aggregator citing the wiki; the wiki itself was not reachable.

## Relationship between TTK, target speed, hitbox size and engagement distance

### Takeaway
I found no published quantitative "tracking difficulty" model, but developers' own explanations converge: long TTK is only acceptable when the shooter has a real chance to miss and the target a real chance to act, and each studio buys that with a different lever (movement in Apex and Quake, bigger projectiles in Overwatch, precision multipliers in Destiny and Halo-likes, portals in Splitgate). When the lever is too strong (Apex mobility) or absent (sponge complaints in Overwatch S9, Warzone) the TTK gets moved.

### Cited Findings
- Respawn (Apex): higher TTK made players "value mobility too much over tactical positioning"; the fix was to lower TTK through weapon damage. — [EA designers' notes 28.0](https://www.ea.com/games/apex-legends/apex-legends/news/28-0-designers-notes)
- 1047 Games (Splitgate, 2019): "We wanted a TTK (time-to-kill) that was long enough that players had time to use portals to strategically flank", but not so long that enemies could escape through a portal every time they were hit. An Edge feature (2021) reports the same intent: time-to-kill akin to Halo's, enough time to make moves without making it easy to run away when about to die. — [Unreal Engine developer interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/developer-interviews/splitgate-arena-warfare-infuses-elements-of-halo-and-portal-to-create-a-new-breed-of-fps), [Edge via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/australia/edge/20211007/281741272586342) (both search summaries; pages blocked)
- Blizzard (Overwatch 2 S9) raised projectile radii by 0.05-0.15 m and raised health at the same time, explicitly as a pair: bigger projectiles raise accuracy and damage output, so health goes up to compensate; the stated net effect is less randomness and less burst. After launch: "accuracy numbers are meeting intentions but some are pushed a bit too far". — [Blizzard patch notes](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2024/02), [esports.gg](https://esports.gg/news/overwatch/overwatch-2-gets-projectile-size-and-hero-health-adjustments)
- Riot (Valorant): with very low TTK, 20-50 ms decides gunfights; skilled players noticed 10 ms changes; a 10 ms change in peeker's advantage flipped an Operator-holder vs rifle-peeker matchup from 90% one way to 90% the other. Peekers had about 141 ms more than holders to react in the baseline model, cut by about 40 ms with routing and to about 71 ms at 144 FPS; Riot told the press under 80 ms was "fair ... with our weapons and tuning". Movement inaccuracy and hit tagging exist to hand time back to the holder. — [Riot netcode article](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/peeking-valorants-netcode), [PC Gamer interview](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/valorant-peekers-advantage-interview) (search summary)
- Riot's Phantom vs Vandal data drop (4M+ matches): when a Phantom headshot at 15-30 m deals 140 and fails to kill, the Phantom player still wins the duel about 78% of the time, the Vandal player about 11%, a teammate finishes about 10%. Researcher's conclusion: "game sense determines a win more than raw accuracy." — [Riot data drop](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/dev/valorant-data-drop-phantom-vs-vandal/)
- PC Gamer on Splitgate 2 (60/100): "a quick time-to-kill and negligible recoil" make aim the main way to score kills, the arsenal is near-identical carbines and scoped rifles with few power weapons worth fighting over, and the reviewer wished the battle royale's longer TTK carried over to arena. — [PC Gamer review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/splitgate-2-review) (search summary; page truncated on fetch)
- Embark (The Finals): too wide a health spread across body types (150-350) makes weapons hard to balance, and speed differences split teams by 5-10 s on arrival. — [Respec Order notes](https://www.reachthefinals.com/patchnotes/respec-order)
- Ubisoft's XDefiant team described TTK as a depth tool: they hoped a longer TTK creates "a larger skill gap for players to progress on" and noted abilities can give players a chance to escape a fight alive. — [Ubisoft XDefiant TTK debrief](https://ubisoft.com/en-gb/game/xdefiant/news/lEirmItIsbN8JxJFEDSus/xdebrief-timetokill-ttk) (search summary; page redirected on fetch)
- Tripwire's John Gibson (on Call of Duty): compressing the skill gap is a deliberate design choice made by adding randomness. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/uk/call-of-duty-red-orchestra-2-interview) (search summary)
- Player-level argument (Apex forum, opinion): longer TTK gives the player being shot more time to react to being outplayed, compressing the first-shot advantage; Halo is cited as a case where headshot accuracy carried a very high skill ceiling. — [EA forum thread](https://forums.ea.com/discussions/apex-legends-feedback-en/so-why-do-people-want-low-kill-times/8435333/replies/8435384) (search summary)
- Respawn GDC 2017 (Titanfall 2 titans): Titan fights "could last minutes" compared with typical shooter engagements "below 5 seconds", which is why Titan combat needed its own readable, ability-driven structure. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/video-how-respawn-evolved-the-titan-combat-in-i-titanfall-2-i-) (search summary)

### Inferences
- A simple way to compare games is angular target speed = strafe speed / distance, and "body-width time" = target width / strafe speed (the time the target takes to move out from under a stationary crosshair). With an assumed 0.5 m wide torso: Valorant rifle run 5.4 m/s gives about 93 ms; Overwatch 5.5 m/s about 91 ms; Apex armed walk about 4.4 m/s gives about 114 ms; Apex AR ADS strafe about 2.2 m/s gives about 227 ms. At 10 m those speeds are about 31, 32, 25 and 13 degrees per second. These are my calculations from the cited speeds and an assumed width, not published figures.
- Read against those numbers, the genre's pairings make sense: Apex lets the shooter's own ADS halve their strafe (slower dodging while shooting) but gives targets slides, jumps and legend mobility; Overwatch has fast unpenalised strafes and compensated with larger projectiles; Valorant and CS2 have similar raw speed but remove the tracking problem altogether by making kills near-instant and penalising movement.
- The combinations the sources flag as feeling wrong:
  1. Long TTK plus strong escape/mobility: fights do not resolve, positioning stops mattering (Apex pre-S24; 1047's "not so long that enemies escape every time").
  2. Long TTK plus more health without more ways to miss: "sponge", kills need follow-up, lower skill expression (Overwatch S9 complaints, Warzone health debate, BO7 "spongy" wording).
  3. Short TTK plus negligible recoil plus samey guns: nothing but raw aim decides, weapons do not differentiate (PC Gamer on Splitgate 2).
  4. Very different TTK by range band on the same pool: close range feels unfair while long range feels fine (JGOD on Warzone 2).
  5. Large per-class health and speed spreads: balance and team cohesion problems (The Finals).
  6. Very low TTK plus latency: outcomes flip on about 10 ms, so movement must be penalised (Valorant).
- For a Halo-style game played on mouse without aim assist, the shield-then-headshot structure is the "precision weapon" lever. If body-shot and optimal TTK are close together (as in Splitgate: Arena Reloaded's AR, 0.80 vs 0.91 s) precision adds little, and the fight is decided by who started shooting first. Destiny's 7.3.5 pass deliberately widened the crit/body gap (crit up 10-14%, body down 3-5%).

### Gaps
- No published tracking-difficulty analysis (strafe speed vs hitbox width vs range) was found from a developer, an aim-trainer vendor or a competitive player. Search results were forum opinion and marketing content.
- Hitbox dimensions for any of these games were not retrieved. Overwatch's projectile-radius numbers are the only hit-volume figures found.
- Typical engagement distances: only Valorant's qualitative split (about half of Phantom kills inside 15 m) was found.
- I found no source explicitly documenting "long TTK plus slow targets means first shot wins / team-shot dominates" outside Halo discourse (covered by another researcher) and forum opinion.
- GDC talks on gunplay (Apex, Overwatch, Valorant, Doom) were not retrieved.

## Weapon handling: hitscan vs projectile, spread and recoil, ADS, falloff, swap and reload

### Takeaway
Quake is the no-recoil, no-ADS, no-falloff extreme with a hitscan/projectile mix; CS2 and Valorant put the skill into recoil patterns and standing still; Apex, The Finals and Call of Duty use ADS with recoil patterns, fast projectiles or hitscan, and range falloff; Overwatch tunes hit volumes directly. Splitgate sits nearest Quake/Halo: little recoil, per-weapon falloff on automatics only, fast reloads.

### Inferences
- Swap speed scales with TTK culture: Splitgate's 0.2-0.3 s holster and The Finals' 0.3 s equip are far quicker than Apex's 0.55 s holster plus 0.6 s deploy. A long-TTK game with slow swaps removes the "shield-break then swap" finisher that arena shooters rely on.
- Reload length relative to TTK: Splitgate's AR reloads in about two body-shot TTKs (1.49 s vs 0.72 s); Apex's R-301 takes 2.4-3.2 s against a roughly 1 s TTK. Long reloads in long-TTK games are part of why one-magazine kills (magazine size vs shots-to-kill: R-301 has 21 rounds for a 14-shot kill) are a tuning target.

- CS2 standing, moving and first-shot inaccuracy values for the AK-47; AWP and M4 handling numbers.
- Projectile speeds for Overwatch, Destiny, The Finals and Splitgate weapons.
- Weapon swap and reload times for Valorant, Overwatch, Destiny, Call of Duty, Titanfall 2.
- Whether Splitgate's primary weapons are hitscan (widely assumed) was not confirmed in a source.

- Quake design consequence: holding mega and armour "keeps your stack full and denies your opponent a fair fight"; item timing is described as the fundamental duel skill. — [Dignitas](https://dignitas.gg/articles/how-to-master-quake-spawn-timers-controlling-mega-and-armor)
- Apex made healing faster in the same patch that lowered TTK; coverage tied the two together. — [Dexerto](https://dexerto.com/apex-legends/apex-legends-makes-huge-changes-to-shield-helmets-and-healing-in-season-24-3057202)

### Inferences
- Ratio of recovery delay to TTK: Overwatch about 5 s to a roughly 1+ s kill; The Finals 8-10 s to about 1.1 s; Apex 2.5 s per 25 shield (10 s to rebuild a purple shield from cells) to about 1 s. In all three, a disengaged player needs several TTKs' worth of safety to reset, so a third party or a pursuing opponent usually arrives first. That is the mechanism that keeps long-TTK fights decisive; if a new game's shield recharge starts quickly relative to its TTK and its movement lets players break line of sight, fights will tend not to finish.
- Games with no escape tools and no regen (CS2, Valorant) resolve the same problem with rounds; games with regen and no rounds need either slow regen or strong pursuit tools.

### Gaps

- Quake Live and Quake Champions item timers from a primary source (QL is commonly described with Q3's 25 s armour / 35 s mega; the one explicit "35 / 25" result was a low-authority summary and another guide says 30 / 30).

## Spawn and respawn timers, round vs respawn structure, and 1v1 / 2v2 formats

### Takeaway
Small-team modes in these games are either round-based with no respawn (CS2 Wingman, Valorant) or respawn modes that make deaths cost more as a round goes on (Splitgate Takedown) or that revolve around a contested resource (Quake duel items). Published numbers on respawn timers are thin.

### Cited Findings
- Quake III spawn rules: respawn at 125 health with machinegun and gauntlet; the spawn point is chosen by sorting all deathmatch spawns by distance from where you died, discarding any that would telefrag, and picking at random from the farther half ("rnd = random() * (numSpots / 2)"). The older "avoid the nearest spot, pick any other at random" logic is commented out. — [g_client.c](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/id-Software/Quake-III-Arena/master/code/game/g_client.c)
- Quake duel is defined by the two major items (mega health and the big armour); controlling them is what makes one player "stacked" against a fresh spawn. — [Dignitas](https://dignitas.gg/articles/how-to-master-quake-spawn-timers-controlling-mega-and-armor)
- CS2 Wingman: 2v2, first to 9 rounds (maximum 16; sides swap after 8; 8-8 is a draw), single-bombsite maps, its own timer and economy rules; matches take about 15-20 minutes. An October 2023 guide gives a 1:30 round timer. — [csdb.gg Wingman guide](https://csdb.gg/guides/what-is-wingman-cs2/), [BLAST](https://blast.tv/article/cs2-wingman-mode-explained) (search summaries)
- Splitgate (2021) ranked: Ranked 4v4 and Ranked Takedown (3v3), with Ranked 2v2 added in a later patch for players who could not assemble a full team. — [esports.net](https://www.esports.net/wiki/guides/splitgate-ranks/), [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/splitgate-gets-ranked-2v2-mode-with-new-patch/1100-6495374/) (search summaries)
- Splitgate: Arena Reloaded Takedown: 4v4, first to three rounds; you respawn after a delay that increases each time you die; each kill by an ally shortens teammates' timers; a team loses the round if all its players are dead at once; timers are visible to both teams and reset each round. — [Takedown wiki](https://splitgatearenareloaded.wiki.gg/wiki/Takedown)
- Splitgate players are split on respawn pacing: one argued fast respawns make fights "repetitive and less meaningful"; a Steam request asked for longer timers because they are "too quick". — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/677620/discussions/0/3052863612131482958) (search summary)
- Destiny 2 raised the 3v3 Clash respawn timer from 5 s to 7 s in Update 7.3.5. — [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/139002/destiny-2-update-7-3-5-patch-notes)
- Splitgate: Arena Reloaded's Classic Arena uses even starts with a fixed loadout (assault rifle + carbine), replacing Splitgate 2's faction loadouts. — [launch overview](https://www.splitgate.com/news/splitgate-arena-reloaded-launch-overview)

### Inferences
- Quake's farthest-half random spawn is the minimal fair 1v1 spawn rule: it prevents spawn-killing without being predictable. Its fairness problem in duel comes from stack asymmetry (125 / 0 vs up to 200 / 200), not from the spawn point.
- Every small-team format found adds a snowball brake or a reset: rounds (Wingman), escalating respawn with kill-refunds (Takedown), or even starts (Classic Arena). A 1v1 / 2v2 respawn mode with regenerating shields and even starts has no stack asymmetry, so readability rests on spawn placement and respawn delay relative to shield recharge.

### Gaps
- Actual respawn times for Splitgate, Overwatch 2, The Finals, Apex modes, Call of Duty and Krunker were not retrieved.
- Apex 1v1 / Arenas, Valorant small-team modes, Overwatch small formats: no results in the searches run.
- Quake Live's duel-specific spawn logic (as opposed to Q3's shared rule) and Quake Champions duel (champion picks, rounds): not retrieved.
- Evidence about what players say makes 2v2 feel fair (as opposed to format rules): none found beyond the Splitgate respawn threads.

## Aim assist for controllers versus mouse in crossplay

### Takeaway
Between 2024 and 2026 the three biggest crossplay shooters all reduced controller aim assist in mixed-input lobbies, each citing its own data that controllers were winning close-range fights; none removed it. The original Splitgate gave PC players no aim assist at all.

### Cited Findings
- Apex Season 22 (Aug 2024): aim assist for controller on PC reduced 25% (the leaked "magnet" value going 0.4 -> 0.3); console players crossplaying into PC lobbies -18% (-22% in performance mode); console-only lobbies unchanged. Designer Eric Canavese: aim assist is "just too strong in these PC lobbies". — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/apex-legends/aim-assist-nerf-season-22), [Beebom](https://beebom.com/apex-legends-aim-assist-nerfed), [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/?p=1304056) (search summaries)
- Apex 2026: after 10 July 2026 anti-cheat changes aimed at controller configs / RC filters, Respawn's internal data showed average controller accuracy down about 2-4%; Respawn said this is not a direct 2-4% cut in assist strength. No further strength cut was confirmed for Season 30. — [GameRiv](https://gameriv.com/apex-legends-controller-aim-assist-nerfed-as-accuracy-drops-2-4/), [AltChar](https://www.altchar.com/game-news/apex-legends-controller-aim-assist-nerf-causes-2-4-accuracy-drop-aHhGZ9M54yRi), [EA forum blog](https://forums.ea.com/blog/apex-legends-game-info-hub-en/anti-cheat-and-aim-assist-update---1782026/13651832) (search summaries; EA page blocked)
- Call of Duty Black Ops 7 (Nov 2025): Treyarch said "controller players are winning slightly more in close-range engagements" and made aim assist "less forgiving". Rotational aim assist now needs the right stick to be actively tracking the target (previously strafing with the left stick was enough); without it the effect is reduced, not removed, and a later update set the reduction to 25% instead of 35%. The range for full strength was extended and long-range strength slightly increased; Season 1 increased long-range strength again; Raven planned matching Warzone changes. Some players said aim assist "barely feels like it's there". — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/call-of-duty/controller-players-are-winning-slightly-more-in-close-range-engagements-treyarch-throws-a-live-grenade-into-controller-vs-mouse-debate-says-aim-assist-will-be-less-forgiving-in-black-ops-7), [Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/call-of-duty/black-ops-7-warzone-aim-assist-changes-3272835), [FinalBoss](https://finalboss.io/treyarch-just-backtracked-on-black-ops-7s-aim), [PC Gamer Season 1](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/call-of-duty/weve-got-some-big-changes-coming-in-season-1-call-of-duty-black-ops-7s-design-director-talks-buffing-aim-assist-adjusting-spawn-locations-and-a-drone-pod-nerf-ahead-of-the-next-season) (search summaries)
- The Finals patch 1.4.1 (Jan 2024): zoom-snapping angular velocity capped (stopping rapid 90-degree snaps); zoom snapping removed from the SR-84, revolver, LH1 and all shotguns; aim assist ignores invisible players; PC clients running key-remapping programs lose aim assist (anti-spoofing). — [PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/the-finals-drops-major-nerf-on-controller-aim-assist-targets-controller-spoofing-on-pc), [TechRadar](https://www.techradar.com/gaming/pc-gaming/the-finals-latest-patch-makes-aim-assist-less-effective) (search summaries)
- Splitgate (2021): aim assist applies only to console players; PC players have none; it is described as light (the crosshair "will very lightly follow" a nearby enemy); the only way for PC players to avoid it was turning crossplay off. Players asked for weaker aim assist and a ranked crossplay toggle; 1047 reportedly had no plans to remove it. — [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/126314/how-does-aim-assist-work-in-splitgate), [Gfinity](https://www.gfinityesports.com/splitgate/aim-assist/) (search summary), [Vaporlens review summary](https://vaporlens.app/app/677620/splitgate)
- Splitgate: Arena Reloaded "corrected aim acceleration" and improved controller feel at relaunch. — [launch overview](https://www.splitgate.com/news/splitgate-arena-reloaded-launch-overview)

### Inferences
- All three studios found the imbalance at close range, where targets cross the screen fastest; that is the regime in which a long-TTK tracking fight is hardest for a mouse and where rotational assist does the most work. A mouse-first long-TTK game without aim assist has nothing absorbing that close-range tracking load, so close-range strafe speed and hitbox size matter more than they do in the console-led games it is modelled on.

### Gaps
- No published accuracy-by-input percentages for Apex, Call of Duty, The Finals or Splitgate were found; only Respawn's "2-4% drop" and Treyarch's "slightly more" statement.
- A Reddit thread with Halo Infinite top-100 accuracy by input exists ([r/halo thread mirror](https://lr.in.psf.lt/r/halo/comments/r3es60/accuracy_stats_for_kbm_vs_controller/)) but could not be fetched, and Halo is out of scope here.

## Field of view, sensitivity and viewmodel conventions

### Takeaway
Defaults sit between about 80 and 103 degrees horizontal with sliders up to 110-120 in most adjustable games, and Valorant and Overwatch cap at 103; the calculator sites I could reach disagree on several defaults and on whether values are horizontal or vertical, so these numbers need checking in-game before they go in a report.

### Cited Findings
- Valorant: fixed 103. One site says this is vertical (about 106 horizontal at 16:9), another lists it as horizontal. — [west-games](https://west-games.com/fps-field-of-view-calculator/); contradicted on axis by [fovcalculator blog](https://fovcalculator.netlify.app/blog/best-fov-for-fps-games) (search summaries)
- Overwatch 2: maximum 103; described by one source as a vertical range of 80-103 and by another as 103 horizontal. — [fovcalculator OW2 page](https://fovcalculator.netlify.app/games/overwatch-2-fov-calculator); contradicted by [fovcalculator blog](https://fovcalculator.netlify.app/blog/best-fov-for-fps-games) (search summaries)
- Apex: maximum 110; default given as 70 by one table and 90 by another. — [west-games](https://west-games.com/fps-field-of-view-calculator/); contradicted by [fovcalculator blog](https://fovcalculator.netlify.app/blog/best-fov-for-fps-games) (search summaries)
- CS2: listed as 90 horizontal by one source; another lists 68 "on 4:3" with a 130 maximum (likely a viewmodel or aspect confusion). — [fovcalculator blog](https://fovcalculator.netlify.app/blog/best-fov-for-fps-games); contradicted by [west-games](https://west-games.com/fps-field-of-view-calculator/) (search summaries)
- Call of Duty (Vanguard / Warzone): horizontal FOV based on 16:9, minimum 60, default 80, maximum 120. — [Esports Tales](https://www.esportstales.com/call-of-duty/best-fov-field-of-view-vanguard), [west-games](https://west-games.com/fps-field-of-view-calculator/) (search summaries)
- Viewmodel / visual recoil as a feel lever: Splitgate: Arena Reloaded reworked visual recoil on all weapons; The Finals 6.0.0 smoothed camera recoil interpolation without changing the pattern. — [launch overview](https://www.splitgate.com/news/splitgate-arena-reloaded-launch-overview), [AKM wiki](https://thefinals.wiki/wiki/AKM)

### Inferences
- Geometry (not from a source): at a fixed distance, raising horizontal FOV from 80 to 103 shrinks a target's on-screen width by roughly a third (ratio of tan(40 degrees) to tan(51.5 degrees) is about 0.67) and makes the player's own movement look faster. A long-TTK tracking game at a high default FOV is therefore asking for more precise tracking on smaller targets than the same numbers at console-style FOV.

### Gaps
- No reliable primary source for default FOV in Splitgate, Quake Champions / Quake Live, The Finals, Destiny 2, Titanfall 2 or Krunker.
- Default sensitivity conventions (cm/360, ADS multipliers) and viewmodel conventions (viewmodel FOV, gun position) were not researched to a citable standard.
- No source found that measures how FOV changes hit rates or perceived speed.

## Splitgate: the commercial "Halo-style on PC" reference

### Takeaway
Splitgate (2021) was 100 regenerating health with no shield, a roughly 0.7 s automatic-rifle and 1.0-1.1 s precision-weapon body TTK, sprint and a weak jetpack on Halo 2-derived movement timings, light console-only aim assist and nearly no recoil. Splitgate 2 (June 2025) sped it up and added factions, abilities and loadouts, was reviewed as having "nothing special" shooting, lengthened TTK twice, and relaunched in December 2025 as Splitgate: Arena Reloaded with factions and abilities removed, even starts, and a slightly longer TTK "to give players more time to react".

### Cited Findings
- Stats (Aug 2021 beta, developer-published damage and fire rates): see the TTK and weapon-handling tables above; 100 HP; AR 720 ms, pistol 744 ms, BR 1000 ms, carbine 1139 ms body-shot TTK; power weapons (rocket, railgun, shotgun, melee bat) one-hit. — [mein-mmo](https://mein-mmo.de/en/splitgate-shows-values-for-the-best-weapons-in-the-hit-shooter-on-steam-ps5-and-xbox,710090), [GamePretty](https://gamepretty.com/?p=40664)
- No shield, a generous regenerating health pool, and a "relatively long time-to-kill"; reviewers called combat and movement "incredibly similar to Halo", closest to Halo 3 in handling but with Halo 4 / 5-style sprint. — [GamePro.de](https://www.gamepro.de/artikel/splitgate-vs-halo,3373234.html), [Giant Bomb user article](https://giantbomb.com/user-articles/id/451413/splitgate-really-is-just-halo-but-with-portals) (search summaries)
- Movement: built on measured Halo 2 timings; sprint always available but modest; jetpack is a short upward burst on a cooldown, intended mainly as fall recovery. — [Last Minute Continue](https://lastminutecontinue.com/review/hands-on-splitgate/), [Push Square guide](https://www.pushsquare.com/guides/splitgate-guide-tips-tricks-and-how-to-play) (search summaries)
- TTK intent (Ian Proulx, 2019): long enough to use portals to flank, not so long that a hit player can always portal away. — [Unreal Engine interview](https://www.unrealengine.com/en-US/developer-interviews/splitgate-arena-warfare-infuses-elements-of-halo-and-portal-to-create-a-new-breed-of-fps) (search summary)

- Why 1047 left Splitgate 1: players stayed "around three to four weeks" and then "would run out of things to do"; a content season took about seven months; the codebase suited about 20 people. — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/-we-were-just-fighting-to-keep-the-lights-on-1047-games-founder-on-ditching-splitgate-to-create-a-scalable-sequel-that-can-prosper)
- Splitgate 2 design: three factions with abilities and faction weapons, loadouts and attachments, 4v4; Proulx stressed that every faction has "the same health ... the same time to kill ... the same movement". Previews placed it "more in between Call of Duty and Halo" in speed, gunplay and TTK, with TTK "fast enough to not be arduous"; GamesRadar's headline: "faster and more complex, but portals aren't the star of the show anymore". — [Dexerto](https://dexerto.com/gaming/splitgate-2-ceo-announces-major-prerelease-plan-to-overcorrect-meta-2874439) (search summary), [GamingTrend preview](https://gamingtrend.com/previews/splitgate-2-hands-on-preview-stepping-through-into-2/), [GamesRadar](https://gamesradar.com/games/fps/splitgate-2-is-faster-and-more-complex-but-portals-arent-the-star-of-the-show-anymore)
- Splitgate 2 tuning during testing: alpha feedback led the studio to experiment with a longer TTK; Public Playtest #1 lengthened TTK 20% for all weapons and reduced recoil. — [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/splitgate-2/closed-alpha-changes-new-maps) (search summary), [Carbine wiki](https://splitgatearenareloaded.wiki.gg/wiki/Carbine)
- Splitgate 2 reception: PC Gamer 60/100 ("its portals don't add much to the genre and its shooting is nothing special"; quick TTK and negligible recoil; weak, samey arsenal; liked the battle royale's longer TTK more); IGN 7/10. Aggregated player reviews: the most frequent complaint was that the sequel "fails to improve on the original" (weight 0.79), with short TTK called frustrating by some and "well-tuned for an arena shooter" by others; movement (slide, jetpack, portals) was a highlight (0.38). A 2024 Steam thread asked whether the game was "too fast for its own good". — [PC Gamer review](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/splitgate-2-review) (search summary), [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/17765/splitgate-2), [Vaporlens summary](https://vaporlens.app/app/2918300/splitgate_2_beta) (machine-aggregated), [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2918300/discussions/0/4428814589387077626)

- Current Arena Reloaded AR numbers: 100 health; Trailblazer about 527 RPM, 9 body shots or 6 head + 2 body, 0.80 s optimal / 0.91 s body; Tempo 0.74 s; Synapse 0.81 s. — [Trailblazer wiki](https://splitgatearenareloaded.wiki.gg/wiki/Trailblazer), [Health wiki](https://splitgatearenareloaded.wiki.gg/wiki/Health)

### Inferences
- Splitgate's answer to "Halo on a mouse" was to drop the shield layer and keep one health bar, keep TTK at the short end of the long cluster (0.7-0.9 s for automatics), remove nearly all recoil, and let precision weapons be only modestly better with headshots (carbine 40 vs 30, AR 18 vs 13). It never published or discussed a mouse-specific tuning philosophy that I could find.
- The direction of travel across four years is informative: Splitgate 1 about 0.72 s AR body TTK; Splitgate 2 launched faster; two successive lengthenings brought Arena Reloaded back to 0.80-0.91 s. The studio's stated reason both times was reaction time and "meaningful gunfights". The band a mouse-led Halo-like has converged on commercially is therefore roughly 0.75-0.9 s for the starting automatic, not Halo's console-era figures.
- The critique that stuck to Splitgate 2 was not "TTK too long" or "too short" as such but that low recoil plus quick kills plus near-identical weapons left nothing distinctive in the gunplay, and that additions (factions, abilities, loadouts) diluted even-start arena play. The relaunch removed the additions rather than re-tuning them.

## Krunker and browser shooters

### Takeaway
Reliable numbers for Krunker are scarce: class health (100 for the default rifle class, 60 for the sniper), slide-hop movement as the core skill, and an unverified 30-60 Hz tick claim. The firmest browser-specific finding is on input: raw, unaccelerated mouse input in pointer lock is available and recommended for FPS games.

### Cited Findings
- Krunker classes: Triggerman (assault rifle, 100 HP) is the most-used; Hunter (sniper, 60 HP) is the glass cannon with one-shot potential; Run N Gun (SMG) and Agent (dual Uzis) are the fastest gun classes with wall-jumping; Rocketeer supports rocket-jumping; Bowman's arrows have travel time. — [Krunker official class guide](https://krunker.io/guides/classes), [shapes.inc classes](https://shapes.inc/fandom/krunker/classes) (fan page, unverified numbers)
- Movement: base speed is slow, slide-hopping is the expected way to move, with about a quarter-second landing window; physics were reportedly once frame-rate dependent (disputed whether still true). — [WePC](https://www.wepc.com/gaming/how-to-slide-hop-in-krunker/), [shapes.inc deep dive](https://shapes.inc/fandom/krunker/deep-dive); contradicted by [speedrun.com guide](https://www.speedrun.com/krunker/guide/vm2um)
- Tick rate: one low-credibility site claims 30-60 Hz "depending on server load"; no official figure found. Krunker is run by FRVR, whose job listing describes a Node.js microservices backend. — [onlinegames.it.com](https://onlinegames.it.com/blog/krunker-io-competitive-scene-2025) (unverified), [FRVR careers](https://careers.frvr.com/jobs/3708899-senior-engineer-krunker)
- Browser mouse input: requestPointerLock({ unadjustedMovement: true }) disables OS mouse acceleration and delivers raw deltas, so the same physical movement always gives the same rotation; web.dev recommends it for FPS games. It shipped first in Chromium (ChromeOS, macOS 10.15.1+, Windows; Linux later). — [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/disable-mouse-acceleration), [MDN requestPointerLock](https://developer.mozilla.org/docs/Web/API/Element/requestPointerLock)
- Known pointer-lock defects: a Chrome bug produced occasional huge deltas (view "teleports"), for which unadjustedMovement was a workaround; a 2026 Firefox bug had pointerrawupdate leaking cursor-recentre corrections on Windows, over-accumulating movement at high polling rates (closed about a month before this research). — [web.dev](https://web.dev/articles/disable-mouse-acceleration), [Mozilla bug 2055058](https://bugzilla.mozilla.org/show_bug.cgi?id=2055058) (search summaries)
- For comparison with a native tactical shooter: Riot runs 128 Hz simulation on client and server with a fixed timestep independent of render rate, targets 35 ms ping for 70% of players, and models 2 server frames plus 3 client frames of buffering. — [Riot netcode article](https://www.riotgames.com/en/news/peeking-valorants-netcode)

### Inferences
- A browser shooter cannot assume raw input: Safari and older Firefox behaviour around unadjustedMovement, plus the two defects above, mean "aim feels off" in a browser build can be an input-path problem (acceleration applied, or spurious deltas) rather than a tuning problem. This is worth ruling out before touching combat numbers; the netcode/feedback researcher may have more.

### Gaps
- Krunker damage values, fire rates, TTK, movement speeds, respawn time, FOV defaults: none found in a reliable source.
- Krunker's netcode model (client-side hit detection vs server rewind, tick and send rates): no first-party source found.
- Other browser shooters (for example Shell Shockers, Venge, War Brokers, Kour): not researched.
- No measured input-latency comparison between pointer lock in a browser and native raw input was found.
